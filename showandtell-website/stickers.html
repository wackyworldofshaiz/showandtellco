Goodto know

How big are the stickers?

About 7 to 8 cm on the longest side. The long sayings, like Eko O Ni Baje, are the widest.

Where can I stick them?

Laptops, water bottles, phone cases, notebooks and suitcases. Stick them on clean, dry, smooth surfaces.

Why are some stickers in two packs?

The danfo and Las Gidi are in both Lagos Life and Japa Edition 2, because no Lagos pack is complete without them.
