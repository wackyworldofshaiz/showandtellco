Frequently asked questions

How do I request a corporate gifting quote?

Fill in the enquiry form above with how many gifts you need, your budget and your deadline. We’ll come back to you with a quote, usually within two working days. You can also message us on WhatsApp.

How much does corporate gifting cost?

It depends on the products you choose, how many you need and any branding. Once we have your enquiry we’ll send a tailored quote, with pricing for your quantity.

Which products can I choose?

Anything in the shop: puzzles, Story Kits, totes, tees, cards, and patches, prints and pins. We can also put together a gift set, for example a tote with a card and a few patches.

Can you add our logo or personalise gifts?

Yes. We can add your logo or each person’s name to totes, tees and cards, and design a custom patch or print for your team. Branded orders take a little longer, so let us know your deadline when you enquire.

How will the gifts be delivered?

We can deliver everything to one address, like your office or event venue, or send each gift straight to each person. Let us know which in the form.
