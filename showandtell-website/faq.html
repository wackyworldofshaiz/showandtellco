Caring for your tote

How do I wash a tote with patches or prints?

Spot cleaning is kindest: dab marks with a damp cloth and a little mild soap. When it needs a proper wash:

  1. Take off any pins first.
  2. Turn the tote inside out.
  3. Hand wash, or use a gentle machine cycle, in cold water with a mild detergent. No bleach.
  4. Air dry flat or hanging. Don’t tumble dry: the heat can loosen patches and crack prints.
When can I wash it for the first time?

Wait at least 24 hours after it’s been pressed, so the adhesive on patches and the ink on prints can fully set.

Can I iron my tote?

Yes, from the inside. Turn it inside out and iron on a cotton setting with no steam. Never put the iron straight onto a print or patch; if you need to press near one, put a thin cloth over it first.

The edge of a patch is lifting. What do I do?

You can press it back down at home:

  1. Heat your iron to the cotton setting, with no steam.
  2. Cover the patch with a thin cloth and press firmly for about 30 seconds without sliding the iron.
  3. Turn the tote inside out and press the back of the patch for another 15 to 20 seconds.
  4. Let it cool completely before using it.

For extra hold, add a few stitches around the edge. If it keeps lifting, message us on WhatsApp and we’ll help.

How do I keep the colours bright?

Wash cold and inside out, skip the tumble dryer, and don’t leave your tote in strong direct sun for long stretches. Store it dry, and don’t pack heavy or sharp things against the patches.

How do I look after my pins?

Push the pin through the fabric and click the locking back into place; to move it, press the release on the back. Take pins off before washing, and check the backs now and then so they stay secure.

Can I buy patches and pins without a tote?

Yes. Patches and pins are sold on their own from the design menu. Prints aren’t, because we press them on for you with a professional heat press.

How do I iron a patch on myself?
  1. Heat your iron to the cotton setting, with no steam.
  2. Place the patch, cover it with a thin cloth and press firmly for about 30 seconds without sliding the iron.
  3. Turn the item inside out and press the back for another 15 to 20 seconds.
  4. Let it cool completely, and wait 24 hours before the first wash.

For extra hold, add a few stitches around the edge.
