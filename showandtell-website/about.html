About us

Everyday joy, printed to keep

Show and Tell Co is a small print studio between Abuja and Barcelona. We take the everyday moments everyone recognises and print them onto things you can give, wear, play with and keep. Whatever we make next, it should feel like something worth sharing, displaying and holding on to.

Whyshow and tell?

Think of the photos on your mum's wall, the aso-ebi everyone wears to the wedding, the fridge covered in magnets from every trip. Some things we show.

Then there's the joke that gets retold at every family gathering, the card kept in a drawer for years, the story behind your favourite shirt. Some things we tell. We make things for both.

Some things you Show The things you wear and put on display. Totes and tees with your own mix of prints, patches and pins. Prints for the wall. Collectibles for the shelf. Howwe make things

  1. 01

    The everyday, made special

    Jollof, danfos, pure water, the family fridge. We start from the small things everyone recognises, then give them a joke, a twist or a little gold.

  2. 02

    Printed with care

    Our cards are printed, and the lettering inside is done by hand when you ask for it. Prints and patches are pressed on by us, one bag at a time.

  3. 03

    Made to be passed on

    We want things to outlast the occasion: the tote that gets a new patch every year, the card that ends up on the fridge.

A note from the team

We started Show and Tell because the things we love most are the small ones: a joke only your family gets, a bus you've ridden a hundred times, a card someone kept for years. We wanted to make things that hold on to those moments, and look good doing it.

Thank you for being here. Whatever you choose, we hope it ends up somewhere it gets shown off, or talked about.

With love,
