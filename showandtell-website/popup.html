    Goodto know

    Do I need to sign up to visit the shop?

    No. The shop is open to everyone, so come and browse. Signing up is only for a spot at the live patch bar.

    Is my spot confirmed when I sign up?

    Not straight away. Spots go in the order people sign up, and we confirm yours on WhatsApp or by email within a couple of days.

    Can I bring something that isn't a tote?

    Yes: tees, sweatshirts, denim jackets and canvas bags all work well. Avoid nylon, waterproof or very stretchy fabrics, which don't take heat well.

    What if the spots are gone?

    Join the waitlist with the same form. If someone can't make it, we'll message the next person on the list.

    Can I book the patch bar for my own event?

    Yes. We bring the patch bar to birthdays, bridal showers and team events in Abuja and Barcelona. Message us on WhatsApp and tell us about it.
